# Music doucumentation

## inspiration
- portal 2 ost (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oAg4yjEmerU&list=PLC32EFD96F3E72D4E)
- soma ost (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LCYJAWP2cs&list=PLC2JRJDXgYZA2nEwz0MDIyVGZnQaIFCT6&index=1)

## characteristic of ost
- ambient music for non combat sections
- smoother synths, slow tempo
- different levels have different themes
- combat music
- heavier rougher synths
- high tempo
- energetic, "chaotic"
- dynamic = adapts to conditions in game
- certain upgrades change/add melodies & instruments -> every run feels unique
- high number of enemies add chords and fullness -> more epic
- low ammo/health makes melodies more frantic, shorter tones -> more tense
